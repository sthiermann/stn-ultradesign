## Action reconciliation

Give materially different actions their own row, including child actions inside a feature group. Record the product's terminology before proposing a new label.

| Capability / action ID | Existing label, entry and evidence | Verb, target and scope | Preconditions / role | Result, persistence and reversal | Proposed label and exact entry | Depicted / simulated / implemented / verified / gap | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Inspect toolbars, overflow/context menus, hover/focus controls, selection modes, editors, per-object settings, empty states and shortcuts. Reconcile source branches and stored choices with runtime observation; unknown does not mean absent.

## Independent state and supported combinations

| State or combination ID | Controls and scope | Allowed simultaneous state / dependency | Opening, closing or switching behavior | Resize / return / reload persistence | Proposed behavior and decision | Evidence or unresolved gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Distinguish hiding information from changing the underlying operation. Separate on-object overlays from inspectors, local filters from processing rules, and panel visibility from unread or draft state. Record only combinations the real product supports or the user explicitly requests.

## Demonstrated retrieval

| Walkthrough ID / capability IDs | Familiar user task | Starting location / role / context | Visible route to the action | Safe result and return | Observed evidence / failed or missing step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

For moved controls, demonstrate the route at the reviewed revision rather than pointing only to a mapping table. A synthetic result demonstrates concept behavior, not backend execution. Keep simulation and unfinished coverage in the external review frame.
